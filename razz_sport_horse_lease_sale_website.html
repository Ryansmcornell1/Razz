In the Barn and Pasture: Playful and Affectionate

Barn Favorite

To match her golden bloodlines, Razz has a true heart of gold—she is an absolute joy to be around and ride, and the quintessential barn favorite. An affectionate, playful people pleaser with a wonderful sense of humor, she acts like a goofy baby out in the pasture—playing with her toys and her horse friends. In the barn, she loves being the center of attention and will happily play along with anything, whether that means letting you dress her up in a funny Halloween costume, pet and groom her for hours, or even give her three baths in a single day.

Sweetest Barn Habit She has several favorite ticklish spots and adores being scratched behind the ears—if you pause, she might even nudge your shoulder to say, “Hey, don’t stop! I was enjoying that; keep going.”

Under Saddle: Focused, Forgiving & Versatile

In the Arena

However, the moment she enters the arena, she shifts into a focused, obedient partner who pays close attention to what her rider wants and tries to collaborate to make everything look smooth and polished. She is tolerant, level-headed, and endlessly forgiving, she stays calm and patient when a rider makes a mistake, loses their balance, or gives mixed signals, never getting frustrated while riders practice and learn new skills. Flexible, gentle, and remarkably easy-going, Razz adapts seamlessly to whoever is in the saddle.

ON THE FLAT

Razz offers a smooth, comfortable ride with natural balance and an adjustable, rhythmic stride—making her an ideal teacher for polishing walk, trot, and canter work.

OVER FENCES

Easily clears 3’ with style and composure—giving young or inexperienced riders a safe, confidence-building feel while offering the athleticism and responsiveness a semi-pro appreciates.

International Jumping Royalty with an Amateur-Friendly Mind

Razz is a striking 16.3h, 5-year-old chestnut KWPN/Oldenburg mare representing a premier cross of international jumping royalty. Sired by the highly sought-after Oldenburg stallion Diamant’s Legacy—a son of the legendary Selle Français stallion Diamant de Semilly—and out of the KWPN mare Gingerloma MG, Razz boasts a golden pedigree.

WHY THIS CROSS MATTERS

Bred from generations of medal-winning athletes and top-rated European breeding stock, Razz combines world-class jumping scope and athleticism with a rare, amateur-friendly mind.

THE BEST OF BOTH WORLDS

Her pedigree brings together the power and careful technique prized in top-level jumpers with the rideability, balance, and movement that make a horse enjoyable to develop every day.

The Sire: Diamant’s Legacy

Oldenburg Stallion

A licensed Oldenburg International stallion, Diamant’s Legacy is both an accomplished competitor and a proven producer passing on scope, athleticism, and rideability.

  • ✓ Proven Show Ring Record: A 1.50m+ Grand Prix winner and Hunter Derby champion who has claimed multiple Welcome Prix victories at the World Equestrian Center in Ocala.
  • ✓ German Jumping Royalty on Dam Line: Traces directly to 1.60m Grand Prix standout Couleur Rubin as well as Grannox.
  • ✓ Notable Offspring: Celebrated for passing on a willing temperament suitable for both professionals and amateurs—including approved young stallions Diamant’s Invincible NA and Toasty AF, and 1.30m jumper Diamant’s Zoe NA.

The Grandsire: Diamant de Semilly

Selle Français Legend

On her paternal side, Razz carries the direct influence of her legendary grandsire, Diamant de Semilly—the powerhouse Selle Français stallion (also approved with the Oldenburg studbook) widely regarded as one of the greatest show jumping sires in modern history.

  • ✓ #1 Sire in the World: Ranked #1 on the WBFSH Sire Rankings in 2015 and 2016, siring Olympic & 1.60m superstars including Emerald, Dominator 2000 Z, Don VHP Z, Quickly de Kreisker, and Mumbai.
THE “DIAMANT” BLUEPRINT

The legendary Diamant line is associated with exceptional scope, careful jumping technique, athleticism, and the competitive drive to succeed at the highest levels.

A CHAMPION’S LEGACY

Ridden by Éric Levallois, Diamant de Semilly won Gold at the 2002 World Equestrian Games, Silver at the 2003 European Championships, and the 2002 French National Championship outright.

The Dam: Gingerloma MG

KWPN Mare

Razz’s dam brings the prestigious Dutch "Loma" maternal line to this pairing, uniting two influential KWPN sires renowned for producing dual-purpose jumping and dressage athletes.

  • ✓ Olympic & Performance Pedigree: Sire Navarone is by double 1996 Olympic Gold Medalist Jus de Pomme; dam Miloma is by Goodtimes (descendant of Nimmerdor & Caletto I), a Dutch test champion and USDF Dressage Sire of the Year producer famed for elastic movement and jumping ability.
  • ✓ Breed-Show Champion: Gingerloma MG earned 1st place at the Brookside Breed Show, 1st place at the NEDA Fall Show (78.5%), and Reserve Champion in the USDF Breeders Championship Series Final.
  • ✓ Elite Maternal Family: Consistently produces top-rated KWPN offspring meeting strict standards for conformation, movement and health—excelling as 1.30m+ jumpers and competitive dressage horses.
Genetics & Performance

The Ideal Modern Sport Horse Formula

When you combine Diamant’s Legacy and Gingerloma MG, you get the ideal modern sport horse formula: FRENCH power and scope (via Diamant de Semilly) balanced with DUTCH rideability, movement, and soundness (via Navarone and Goodtimes).
